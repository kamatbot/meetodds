A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Clear answers.
No fine-print energy.

A little more about your data, your AI, and getting started.

Can everything really stay on my Mac?

Yes. Choose local transcription and Built-in Local AI, or an Ollama instance running on your own Mac, for summaries. Recording, transcription, and summarization can all happen on-device. Download your models first; after that, this local workflow can run offline. Cloud providers are optional.

How does connecting my ChatGPT account work?

In the desktop app, choose OpenAI Codex (ChatGPT subscription) as your summary provider and follow the OpenAI sign-in flow. MeetOdds uses the Codex access available to your eligible ChatGPT account, without a separate API key. Available models and usage limits are set by OpenAI. This website does not sign you in or collect your credentials.

What leaves my device in ChatGPT mode?

The transcript text needed to generate or translate your summary is sent to OpenAI. Audio recording and transcription remain local, but cloud summarization is not offline or fully local. Choose local AI when you need meeting content to stay on-device.

Is MeetOdds free? Do I need an API subscription?

MeetOdds is free and open source. Local AI does not require a paid cloud account or incur cloud AI usage fees. ChatGPT-connected summaries use your eligible account’s Codex access and are subject to OpenAI’s availability and limits. The separate OpenAI Cloud API option uses an API key and is billed separately from a ChatGPT subscription.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. MeetOdds captures microphone and system audio on your Mac, rather than sending a bot to join a call. Enable the necessary macOS audio permissions and make sure participants know and agree that you are recording.

What can I export?

Export meeting documents as PDF, Microsoft Word (DOCX), Markdown, or JSON. Review AI-generated content before sharing: summaries can make mistakes, and the original transcript is there to help you check.
